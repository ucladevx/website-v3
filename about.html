Our Mission

We aim to empower students to bring bold product ideas to life. Through hands-on teamwork, mentorship, and career-building opportunities, we prepare the next generation of innovators to thrive in tech.

Our Story

We started as a project initiative within UCLA's ACM student chapter. We wanted to give student developers team-based experience building real-world software applications and technical "moonshot" projects.

We then became an independent entity outside of ACM in 2017 to expand its real-world software development scope.
